# Volume 03: The Flood Ledger

Chapter range: 101–150 (planned). Batch 0001: Chapters 101–110 (Days 148–157). Batch 0002: Chapters 111–120 (Days 158–167).

## Central pressure

Meltwater Verge is flooding while Cresset remains dry, exposing the false choice between saving one city and abandoning another. The freed water of Volume 02's window stands in a lane on the far bank where a return said *no inhabitants* for nineteen years — a person problem, and a Fair Copy problem.

## Starting state (Chapter 101)

Rural route open but unstable; flume shut and chained (new chain); Sallow Head gate shut, untouched; third rod empty (padlock, new empty line); plate unfitted; Perdie has no book (numbered sheets, column 34½ in seven lines, foot empty, number due in about a year); item one stands printed; 3,485 demand unbacked in district 14 and not withdrawable; requisition to Vellum on the road; 1843 question on a sheet; letter three lines unsent; row number entered for count not supply; Aldis unthanked; fifth rod promised not cut; crew gone; Tarin tied to route, filling a butt evenings, cannot leave.

## Power state

Bend, Stage 3, no advance and no retreat. The change is social: **he can redirect only where people share the risk.** A room of eleven refuses to act on what his hand says and is right (Mon 26th, Ch 103); four residents say *we want it* and the bank foot is eased into the drain (Sun 1st, Ch 109). The difference is never resolved. He has no word for it and gives it none, and he says the people who told him a name is negotiated are right (Ch 110).

## Major locations

Far-bank row at Cinder Ford (six doors, pump, lane, well; the ground four feet under at the flume mouth); the long drain; the plank and butt at Nether Sallow; the tank at Sallow Head; the record house below the culvert; the culvert post and its empty ninety-year-old tin frame; the shop at Brant; Vellum — Memory Office, tally office, reading room, flood court; the cottage at Brant.

## Major factions

Fair Copy under Cael Dorrin (rung three of five; right about a record, wrong about a valley; procedure, a deadline that is somebody else's, and a sentence he believes); local flood wardens; the Water Office; valley assemblies that are not assemblies; the tally office; the Memory Office.

## Escalation (planned)

Dorrin seals to protect a boundary → wave (done 106–107). Bank view and willing tally → what countable costs (Batch 0002). Flood-book provenance (desk-hand, ink batch, date, no return) → the missing-pages pressure without delivering the midpoint (Batch 0002). Midpoint ~Ch 125: the flood ledger shows the Ninefold Flood began with conflicting gate orders. **Set up in Ch 108 by three hands on the front leaves and a question refused; not delivered, and it must not be delivered early.** Climax: spillway, controlled flood on a former military terrace, record house destroyed, Mara secures fragments from residents who never trusted the court. Resolution: floodplain habitable, Dorrin forced to show the legal basis for the unlisting; some land not rebuilt.

## Relationship change (planned)

Mara publishes the valley and holds his sheet. Trust damaged by honesty, not repaired by confession alone. Begun Ch 108–109; condition four holds; nothing repaired. **She is not told the eleven names and is not told how many.**

## Rules carried

The thirteen prohibitions hold (see the close review and `state/batch-0011-summary.md`). No ghost. No tea, no two-finger press. Boryn and the One Map unnamed. Sallow Head gate shut; flume sluice chained; weep under its rind; plate unfitted; third rod empty with the new line empty. Sallow Dyke is not the waste pipe. A name never commands a route. **The volume's own words — *the volume*, *this batch*, *an opening movement* — may not appear in a chapter.**
