## Power state

Bend, Stage 3, no advance and no retreat. The change is social: **he can redirect only where people share the risk.** A room of eleven refuses to act on what his hand says and is right (Mon 26th, Ch 103); four residents say *we want it* and the bank foot is eased into the drain (Sun 1st, Ch 109). The difference is never resolved. He has no word for it and gives it none, and he says the people who told him a name is negotiated are right (Ch 110).

## What Batch 0002 set (Ch 111–120)

**The bank view and the rate.** The Sunday face measured; the finding true and also the instrument of the charge — *a work that is not a making-good is a work of improvement and is charged to the district*. The district entered in the district book **on the twelfth of next month**; the making-good worked **at six in the morning on Saturday the seventh**, which is the hour the drain is lowest and the hour a man who fills a butt is not; the works at **nineteen shillings and twopence** (materials 4s 8d; labour six persons and forty-six hours at threepence the hour, 11s 6d — **at fourpence the total would be exactly a pound, and fourpence was the going rate in the year Corrie came**) and **the rate unquotable, because a rate is a fraction of a valuation that has not been made**; a rate charged on a door and not a person; a door that can be sold for arrears; the valuation in about a year by a man who will not stay the night. **The tally if willing:** eleven persons, six doors, **nine names given, one name withheld by the person — recorded unwilling and not argued with — and one person absent and not named for him because his mother would not be asked**, and the withheld name bought him nothing.

**The flood book, one step and no more:** docket 411, the Receipts desk of the examination table, an establishment list that is a list of offices with the holder column reading *vacant*; court-supply pencil batch eighteen, exhausted inside a fortnight fourteen years ago; **no return ever recorded, because the volume went out on a writ and the writs are in another office in the Low Ward**; a requisition for writ 214 entered and read back by Oriel Fenn and **unanswered at six days**. **No man at the desk is named, the taker is not named, the cut pages are not delivered, and the midpoint is untouched.**

**Bend socially conditioned and not advanced:** the making-good worked at six in the morning, which is the hour the drain is lowest and the hour a man who fills nineteen butts is not; the butt filled at four in the dark and afterwards by four other men, with the shutter saying so; a well and a pump frame and two refusals, one of timing and one of scope. **The frame is still empty on the twelfth, on purpose,** and the only notice this business has produced is on a bar at the ford end, because it names a district and a district is a thing a man can be served.

**The volume's clock is now the record house:** the door hangs open, a writ can be served without warning, eleven people have seen the stubbed page, and not one of them is on anything but a hook. **And the hearing of witnesses is the nineteenth of next month, sixty-one miles east, and Tarin Rusk is not going to it.** The batch view of it: the first eleven persons to be counted where nineteen years said nobody, the first money ever wanted off that lane, the first notice ever nailed anywhere on this account, and a man who has never once been able to say a number out loud.
