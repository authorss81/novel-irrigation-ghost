# Volume 01 Batch 0001 Outline

Chapter range: 1–10

## Batch goal

Prove the series promise in ten chapters: the drought is a lie about infrastructure rather than a natural failure, the power is real but cannot create water, and Tarin's competence is what makes the first miracle survivable. Establish the Standing Ration deadline, the Ashgate anomaly, Glassstep as a living unlisted district, the Gauge's limits, the Tarin–Mara friction, one small victory, and one permanent cost.

## Starting condition

Cresset, three weeks from the midsummer ration review, fourteen months without real rain. Tarin Rusk is a senior flow engineer with a sealed structure to recalibrate and no supernatural ability. Mara Quill is three weeks into a routine provenance check she does not yet know is a mystery. The two have never spoken.

## Starting relationships

Tarin and Mara are professionally suspicious and personally unknown. Niko Venn is Tarin's twenty-two-year-old apprentice. Yara Sen signs his exposure paperwork. Halden Roe is his employer and the man who will end his career. Nobody knows Tarin is from Glassstep.

## Batch midpoint

**Chapter 5.** Tarin and Mara are forced into the same room by a requisition and discover they are each holding half of a physical fact. The Gauge speaks for the first time and asks for a witness. The professional antagonism becomes respect that neither of them wants.

## Batch climax

**Chapter 10.** Tarin names the branch aloud, in public, before the residents of an unlisted district and a sluice warden who is entitled to write it down. The water runs for eleven hours. The accusation against him becomes formal the same afternoon.

## Required character changes

- Tarin goes from a man who reports problems to a man who makes a decision with witnesses and a limit attached. He is still concealing in Chapter 2, which must be visible as a choice, not a slip.
- Tarin must fail to conceal the Chapter 7 cost. He tells Yara before she finds it.
- Mara moves from institutional caution to holding evidence alone, and pays a career cost for it in Chapter 8.
- Halden Roe is established as the volume's antagonist with a motive: falsified Ashgate measurements concealing illegal contractor pumping. He is a liar, not a mage, and must never use magic.
- Niko appears as someone who knows the terrace by body rather than document, and who is better at it than Tarin.

## Required power changes

- **Stage 0 (Ch 1–4):** no magic. Tarin's competence carries the batch.
- **First Gauge statement (Ch 5):** one panel, plain, incomplete, and it asks for a witness rather than offering a solution.
- **Stage 1, Trace (Ch 6):** deliberate first use. Requires a physical trace, a willing witness, and a record he makes with his own hands.
- **First name debt, minor (Ch 7):** a practiced hand motion of twenty years is gone. It does not return in this batch.

## Required world reveals

- Ashgate Reservoir is not empty; the intake holds residual pressure six years after closure.
- Water can run uphill because of a real pressure head. Tarin did not make it.
- Glassstep is inhabited. The residents are not ghosts, echoes, or memories.
- A blind route exists as a category: physically present water with no accepted name, dangerous to force.
- Salt fever is a physical illness caused by badly addressed water.
- The court's own ink was used to remove a pressure figure from the Ashgate log and names from the Glassstep survey, in the same year and the same hand.

## Open questions after the batch

- Where is the uphill water actually coming from? The channel has no upstream.
- Why does the Water Office's own ledger say the reservoir is empty when it is not?
- Who signed the Glassstep redactions, and whose hand was on the Ashgate leaf?
- Can the branch be opened again without a witness, and what happens to the people living on it if it cannot?
- What is the second voice in the Gauge, and why does it sound like a crowd?

## Rules for this batch

- Tarin never creates water. Every drop is residual pressure, groundwater, an existing channel, or the rain.
- Maximum one Gauge panel per chapter, and only in Chapters 5 and 10.
- The ghost does not speak in this batch. The reader should feel pressure and absence, not a guide.
- No flashback longer than a paragraph. Tarin's Glassstep past arrives as recognition, not exposition.
- End on completed beats. No chapter may break off mid-action to manufacture suspense.

## Chapter Cards

### Chapter 0001 — The Gauge at Ashgate

**POV:** Tarin Rusk

**Location/time:** The Ashgate inspection works, upper terrace, dawn, first week of the ration year.

**Immediate goal:** Recalibrate the decommissioned intake gauge and file a clean log before the monthly warden check.

**Resistance:** The gate housing is seized after six years of neglect. The floats will not settle. He is alone, out past the last lamp, on unauthorized time.

**Information revealed:** The intake is not dry. There is residual pressure behind the inspection gate, and the chamber is not the empty ruin every record describes.

**Action or decision:** He forces the inspection gate with a bar and a bad shoulder. A stream runs uphill through a stone channel that has been dry for six years.

**Emotional change:** Professional satisfaction curdles. He has been wrong about a structure he knows well, and he knows exactly how he was wrong.

**Power or resource change:** No magic. He gains one physical fact, a contaminated brass float, and a wet sleeve.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Tarin opens the gate. He does not create, summon, or redirect the water. The pressure head is real and above the gate seating.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** The water is still running downhill to a district the warden's map lists as unoccupied, and he cannot stop it with the tools on site.

### Chapter 0002 — The Warden's Road

**POV:** Tarin Rusk

**Location/time:** The sluice warden's station on the terrace road, midday.

**Immediate goal:** Close out the inspection so the log matches reality, and get back down the hill before anyone asks why the gate is open.

**Resistance:** Sluice warden Pell, a practical man who counts channels rather than stories, and a hydraulic problem: the head is above the gate seating, so the flow cannot be shut from this end.

**Information revealed:** The flow is real, pressurized, and physically unstoppable with what is available. Its destination is a district marked unoccupied on every map Pell owns.

**Action or decision:** He does not report the flow. Reporting it means admitting the sealed gate is open and that he is unauthorized, so he walks the line instead to see where it goes.

**Emotional change:** He conceals. It is a small, ordinary, entirely human cowardice and it must read as a decision rather than a slip.

**Power or resource change:** He loses the option of pretending the day did not happen. He learns the head and the destination.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The concealment is a choice, and the volume will hold him to it in Chapter 9's hearing.

**Ending type:** cost

**Next-chapter pull:** He arrives at a district that should be empty and finds children in the courtyard.

### Chapter 0003 — A District of Blue Doors

**POV:** Tarin Rusk

**Location/time:** Glassstep, the lower terrace, late afternoon.

**Immediate goal:** Find out where the flow is going and stop it before it floods something occupied.

**Resistance:** There is nowhere for it to go. The district has houses, washing, a woman beating a mat, and no name on any sign, warden's map, or tax roll. The water is running into a courtyard cistern that has been dry for years and filling it to a third.

**Information revealed:** Glassstep is inhabited. Tarin grew up here. Nobody here is a ghost, an echo, or a memory.

**Action or decision:** He gives his own name and his father's name to a woman old enough to remember the family, and asks for help instead of trying to fix it alone.

**Emotional change:** Shame and relief together. He is a stranger to a neighborhood he used to live in, and it is worse and better than being blamed for it.

**Power or resource change:** He gains a witness candidate and a reason to care that is not professional.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Blue doors, the mat, the dry courtyard cistern. The cistern stops filling at a third, which matters later and is not explained in this batch.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** The Office wants someone from the Water Office to explain a pressure log for a gate that was closed before the flood, and the requisition points at its most recently inconvenient engineer.

### Chapter 0004 — The Weight of a Leaf

**POV:** Mara Quill

**Location/time:** The Municipal Memory Office in Vellum, provenance bench, mid-morning.

**Immediate goal:** Close a routine re-binding of the Ashgate maintenance volume so the quarter's backlog clears before the review.

**Resistance:** The volume is sound and also not sound. One leaf is a different weight and a different sizing, and its verso holds the pressure shadow of writing that no longer exists above it.

**Information revealed:** A page was removed and replaced. The surviving impression is a pressure log for a gate recorded as closed before the Ninefold Flood.

**Action or decision:** Mara refuses to certify the volume, signs the discrepancy into the requisition book in her own hand, and then keeps working on it after hours, because her own rule says an unverified record must never decide a public route and she wants to know which way this one fails.

**Emotional change:** The pleasure of catching a real problem, and the first cold sense that the problem is larger than a binding error.

**Power or resource change:** She gains one certified discrepancy and one note from her supervisor about her hours.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Mara is non-magical and stays non-magical for the series. Her method is provenance, ink, paper, and pressure shadows.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** The requisition requires the Water Office to explain the pressure log, and the Office points at Tarin.

### Chapter 0005 — Two Professionals, One File

**POV:** Tarin Rusk, with Mara Quill

**Location/time:** The Memory Office, provenance room, mid-morning, four days later.

**Immediate goal:** Answer the requisition with an explanation that does not require him to admit the gate is open.

**Resistance:** Mara has read the pressure log and knows precisely what a real head at a closed gate looks like. She suspects he altered a record. He suspects she intends to seal a working route under cover of historical accuracy. Neither says so directly for most of the scene.

**Information revealed:** The pressure figures match what Tarin felt at the intake, down to a number he wrote only in his own notebook. His private note is now the corroboration that can hang him.

**Action or decision:** He shows her the number, knowing it incriminates him, because a correct record is more use to him than a safe one. She tells him what was removed: the figures were replaced, not the readings.

**Emotional change:** The first moment of professional respect between two people built to be opponents, and neither wants it.

**Power or resource change:** **First Gauge statement.** His hand is on the vault's record stone. One panel, plain and incomplete, naming a physical condition and asking for a witness rather than offering a solution.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The Gauge never states a solution, a probability, or a future. It is a condition, and it can be wrong.

**Ending type:** earned hook

**Next-chapter pull:** The Gauge's request is a witness, and the only witness in this city lives in a district that does not legally exist.

### Chapter 0006 — The Low Pump on Glassstep

**POV:** Tarin Rusk

**Location/time:** Glassstep, the low pump, dawn.

**Immediate goal:** Put water into Glassstep's low pump before the cistern runs dry, using the branch he saw in the courtyard.

**Resistance:** The branch is blind. It has no name, no record, and no legitimacy, and he can force it physically — but a forced route runs crosscurrent, and the families on the receiving side are the ones who get sick.

**Information revealed:** Naming a route requires three things he can obtain — a physical trace, a willing witness, a durable record — and a fourth, legitimacy, that cannot be manufactured. Niko Venn appears here, knowing which stones on the terrace are markers and which are only stones.

**Action or decision:** He asks a resident to witness and explains to them what they are agreeing to before he uses them, and he re-cuts a record stone himself with a borrowed chisel before he opens the branch at all.

**Emotional change:** Being trusted by people he failed as a child is worse, and better, than being blamed.

**Power or resource change:** **Stage 1, Trace**, used deliberately for the first time. The branch runs for a bounded window.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Tarin does not use anyone's private memory as an anchor. The refusal in Chapter 39 has to be a real pattern, not a surprise.

**Ending type:** first small victory

**Next-chapter pull:** The pump runs, and the water tastes wrong.

### Chapter 0007 — What the Water Brought With It

**POV:** Tarin Rusk, with Yara Sen

**Location/time:** The Glassstep clinic and the courtyard, daytime.

**Immediate goal:** Establish whether the water in the low pump is safe before anyone drinks more of it.

**Resistance:** Yara Sen finds early salt fever in three children and will not accept that residual water will clear on its own. The crosscurrent is a consequence of his opening, and she says so to his face.

**Information revealed:** Salt fever is physical, fast, and caused by badly addressed water rather than by bad water. Treatment exists and is not simple. The Gauge was right that a route remembered and said nothing whatever about the cost.

**Action or decision:** He closes the branch early to stop the spread, knowing it means the pump runs dry in two days, and he tells Yara the cause before she proves it.

**Emotional change:** He chooses exposure over control for the first time in the volume, then cannot write down the date and cannot work out why his hand will not make the notation he has made ten thousand times.

**Power or resource change:** **First name debt, minor.** A practiced hand motion from twenty years of gate work is gone, and his bitter tea tastes of nothing.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The debt does not heal in this batch. No lost detail is restored later as a convenience.

**Ending type:** cost

**Next-chapter pull:** He needs another window, and only one person in the city has already agreed to be a witness.

### Chapter 0008 — The Replaced Leaf Has a Cousin

**POV:** Mara Quill

**Location/time:** The Memory Office restricted stack, after hours.

**Immediate goal:** Identify the ink on the replaced leaf before her supervisor pulls the volume into restricted handling.

**Resistance:** The volume is flagged, the stack is locked after dark, and the ink turns out to be court-supply stock from a specific year — which means it was applied by an institution and not by a forger.

**Information revealed:** The same substitution pattern appears in the Glassstep survey redactions from twenty-six years ago. Same hand, same year, same ink. On the Ashgate leaf the pressure figures were removed; on the Glassstep documents the residents' names were removed.

**Action or decision:** She hand-draws the page instead of using the office machine, and she does not sign the register. It is a small, deliberate, career-costing act.

**Emotional change:** Fear, and the specific excitement of being the only person in the building who can read the evidence.

**Power or resource change:** She gains a real pattern and a real vulnerability: she is now holding something alone.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The pattern is evidence, not proof of a final villain. The hand belongs to the Commissioner's office, not to a supernatural agent.

**Ending type:** discovery

**Next-chapter pull:** The pattern has a name on it in a hand she learned at her mother's kitchen table, and the Water Office is being asked to explain itself by Monday.

### Chapter 0009 — The Commissioner's Kindness

**POV:** Tarin Rusk

**Location/time:** The Water Office, upper floor, mid-morning.

**Immediate goal:** Get the recalibration log accepted as an ordinary record and keep his position.

**Resistance:** Halden Roe is gracious, reasonable, and has already built the file. The log Tarin filed is now the primary evidence that the Ashgate gate was opened and its figures falsified. Roe offers him a transfer to a district with no problems.

**Information revealed:** The falsified Ashgate measurements are Roe's own work, and they conceal illegal pumping by contractors through a reservoir every ledger says is empty. He is not operating anything ancient. He is lying about arithmetic.

**Action or decision:** He refuses the transfer and accuses Roe of falsification in front of two colleagues, knowing he has neither proof nor standing.

**Emotional change:** He crosses from private guilt into public conflict and discovers that he is willing to be the man in the room who says it out loud, which is a smaller and more useful thing than he imagined.

**Power or resource change:** He loses his desk, his reference, and his access to the district. He keeps his notebook and his tools.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** Roe never uses magic, never learns to, and is not the architect of anything. He is removed, not killed.

**Ending type:** reversal

**Next-chapter pull:** He is formally stood down and charged with tampering with city records, and the ration review is in three weeks.

### Chapter 0010 — One Name, One Window

**POV:** Tarin Rusk

**Location/time:** The Glassstep courtyard and the warden's station, late afternoon, public.

**Immediate goal:** Say the branch's name in front of witnesses before the district's cisterns run out, and be stopped.

**Resistance:** Naming it in public is what makes him the man who opened a sealed reservoir, and warden Pell is entitled to write down whatever he hears. This is the last day before the ration review collapses.

**Information revealed:** The Gauge gives him a rule rather than an answer, and the rule is that a witness must be able to refuse. The refusal is what makes the naming legal, not the consent alone.

**Action or decision:** He asks to be recorded, in a courtyard, with the residents present and free to leave. One of them does leave, and the naming holds anyway. He takes the bounded window he can actually get, states in public that it is temporary, and says plainly that he does not know where the water comes from.

**Emotional change:** Relief and terror in the same breath. He tells the truth about the limits of his knowledge in front of a crowd for the first time and does not soften it.

**Power or resource change:** Water runs. The accusation becomes formal the same afternoon, and the name is now in the record in his own hand.

**Continuity fact to preserve:** The window is eleven hours. It is bounded, temporary, and insufficient, and the volume must not treat it as a solution.

**Ending type:** batch climax

**Next-chapter pull:** The branch keeps running for nine hours after Tarin is removed from the district, and then stops the moment he is gone.
