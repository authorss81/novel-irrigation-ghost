## The five jobs

1. **Hold the bank view and the willing tally without turning either into a victory.** The bank view (Dorrin's procedure) should find the bank partly held by residents' work and say so on paper — a correct paper that helps nobody simply. The tally must come mouth by mouth, with at least one refusal that stands (someone unwilling, recorded as unwilling, not argued with). Countable must cost something visible: a rate discussion, a bill form, or a door that can now be rated.
2. **Advance the pencil line one step and no more.** Memory Office provenance: whose desk-hand, what ink batch, what date — and **no return ever recorded**. Do not deliver the missing pages' content (midpoint belongs ~Ch 125). Do not name the taker. Do not join the flood book to the 1843 book (name 1843 at most once, only to separate).
3. **Keep Bend socially conditioned.** One scene where shared-risk work succeeds modestly (bank making-good, pump washing, well bailing — ordinary work, his hand among others' hands); one scene where knowing-without-showing is refused again. No new technique, sense, range, or name.
4. **Compound the relationship damage without repairing it.** Mara's drawer sheet (33) must exert pressure: someone asks for it, or its absence is noticed, or publishing 31–32 has a consequence for Tarin she did not intend. He must not learn she holds it unless the scene earns it — and if he learns it, it must cost, not heal. **Do not tell Mara the eleven names, and do not tell her how many.**
5. **Fill the butt every evening.** At least four chapters show or name the evening filling; the routine must collide once with a morning demand (bank view, tally, court paper) so the cost is physical, not symbolic. He cannot leave; he may be angry; he may not go.

## What the repair pass on Batch 0001 changed, so that you do not undo it

## Seven more facts, from the second and third repair passes, that a chapter will contradict if it is careless

**An external review ran on Batch 0001 after the first repair and moved six things; a second review after the second repair moved six more and added this seventh. They are all on the page and all of them are the kind of thing a later batch gets wrong by thinking rather than by looking.**

1. **The tank came back the same inch and is standing at 2 ft 6⅛; the fall was an inch and not half.** 2 ft 6⅛ in on the night of the twenty-second, 2 ft 5⅛ in on the Friday morning, 2 ft 5⅞ in on the Monday the twenty-sixth and the Tuesday the twenty-seventh, and **2 ft 6⅛ in again on the Thursday the twenty-ninth and at every reading since — the whole inch back in six days.** `chapters/volume-02/chapter-0099.md` says on the page that it came back the same inch in about a week, and that page governs a later volume, so **no chapter may say the tank stands a quarter low and no chapter may call the fall half an inch.**
2. **Iven Marrow's letter of the second of next month mentions a Fair Copy certificate, not the valley's sheets**, because a letter four days from Bexhill cannot have read three sheets written on the thirty-first. **If he writes again, he cannot have read anything dated after the twenty-ninth.** He may not be thanked, rescued, exposed or made a villain, and his price has not moved.
3. **The Cinder Ford file is nineteen returns in seven hands** — four in the current clerk's, six in Hanne Ilk's, nine in five hands neither can name — and there is a **tally woman at that desk of about thirty-five who said to Mara's face that a name in a box is a name whatever word is beside it.** She is right and she said it first. **A twentieth year has to add a hand or stop.**
4. **Nesta Sarg has told Tarin to his face, once, in public, that he is a day and a half out**, at the fourth trip on the Thursday evening, with the figure on the shutter beside the plank, which she promised on the Tuesday and which Wick read against the same third doorstep he marked on the Tuesday morning. **She said she would not say it again and she will not.**
5. **The flume launder is two miles and four chains long and the flume sluice is what is chained; four hundred yards is the tank to the sluice.** Cinder Ford is over the Sere from Nether Sallow and the row is past it.
6. **The tin frame on the culvert post is still empty and the batch ends with it empty on purpose.** Dorrin wanted a notice in it and got two notices on two bars at the far bank instead. **Putting something in that frame is the moment the empty frame stops being the point — so if you do it, do it deliberately and know what it costs.**
7. **Mara Quill's certificate of Wednesday the twenty-eighth says *unknown* in the box for persons, and says the tally of persons by name is to follow Monday the second of next month if willing.** She refused to enter a figure she had not been given, and the only paper carrying eleven — Corrie Lille's door-by-door count, in a second hand on the valley's Wednesday afternoon sheet — arrived after she had signed. **The certificate never carries the number and never may; the tally this batch writes is the answer to that empty box, and it must come mouth by mouth, with the count belonging to Corrie and not to any instrument.**
