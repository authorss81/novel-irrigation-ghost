## THE FIVE JOBS

**1. Put the water somewhere it has never been, and let a person be standing in it.**

**The flume's outfall is on the long drain, and the long drain runs four miles two chains from its head, and the nineteen households are at the far end of it, and the ground at the mouth of the flume is four feet below the field the water has been standing in for six years.** Volume 02's window did not reach the nineteen houses and the chapter says so three times. **This batch is where it does reach, and the person it reaches is not in the nineteen.**

**The district on the far bank has a return that says *no inhabitants* and has said it for nineteen years. Nobody in Volume 02 has ever mentioned it, and this batch meets it cold, in daylight, in the first chapter or the second, and it meets it as a person and not as a paragraph.** Build it out of ordinary things: a lane, a pump, a row of doors, somebody who has been there nineteen years and was never in a return, a woman who fills a certificate in a city office and has a form with a box that wants a number and a name and there is no name to put in it. **Do not open with a committee. Open with somebody's water going somewhere it was not supposed to go.**

**2. Make Bend a liability, and make the liability social rather than supernatural.**

**He can find the channel with his hand on a wall and there is no way of giving it back, and in Volume 02 that was a gift he could not put down. In Volume 03 it is the reason a room will not listen to him.** He knows where the water is before anybody else does and he cannot show anybody how he knows, and a magistrate who deals in legal recognition is going to treat that as the most dangerous sentence in the basin. **The volume's power change is that he can redirect water only when crews and residents share the risk — so the batch must put him in at least one room where knowing is a disqualification and one place where it saves somebody, and it must not resolve the difference.**

**3. Bring in Cael Dorrin and the Fair Copy, and let the Fair Copy be right about a thing.**

**Cael Dorrin is a Fair Copy magistrate and he is rung three on the ladder, not the final enemy, and the ladder's sentence for him is: he treats legal recognition as proof of physical reality and uses forced relocation as correction.** He must be good at his job in a way that costs him, and he must be arguing from a record that is not a lie, and the record's defect is that nobody has ever gone and looked. **Boryn Vey does not appear and is not named. The One Map is not named. No new final enemy, no second system, no resurrection and no new instrument may be introduced in this batch, and Dorrin is a functionary with a procedure and not a face from the end of the book.**

**The Fair Copy's first act should be sealing something to protect a legal boundary, and it should create a new wave, and the volume's escalation says so.** It is not a villain's move; it is what a man does who has a line on a plan and water on the wrong side of it.

**4. Put the buried record house and the missing pages on the page, in a document, with a date.**

**The old flood ledger shows that the Ninefold Flood began with conflicting gate orders and not with an unmanageable natural disaster, and that is the volume's midpoint reversal and it is NOT this batch's job — this batch must set it up and must not deliver it.** What this batch must deliver is the record house itself as a place people can be sent, and **the first sign of the missing pages, in an ordinary document, in somebody's hand, with a date on it and a reason nobody has written down.** The reader must be able to say at the end of Chapter 110: *there is a book and part of it is gone and somebody with the authority to have taken it has not written down that they took it.*

**5. Write the relationship change, and do not repair it.**

**Mara publishes the missing pages while Tarin admits his old closure, and their trust is damaged by honesty and not repaired by confession alone.** **Mara Quill has not been told about the eleven names and must not be told in this batch.** She is an officer of a city office and not a neutral person, she has entered a row number she had no standing to enter, and she has been in Volume 02 once and refused to certify a discharge as a supply. **Volume 03's pressure is on her, not on him: a flood is the one thing a records office can act on, and this is the first volume in which her profession is the correct response to the emergency, and that is exactly why it is dangerous.** Let her be competent. Let the damage be that being competent is what she does to him.

The throat is three hundred and forty yards from the plank. The long drain is four miles two chains from its head. The flume's outfall is two miles below the plank. The throat is nine feet below the crown of the road and four feet above the long drain.
