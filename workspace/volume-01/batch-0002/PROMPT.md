## The job

## Fixed starting position — Day 21 morning

- The branch stopped at **03:00** on its own, with nobody in it. It stopped at the balance point, not because Tarin was carried out, and it does not come back when he asks.
- Tarin slept in an empty cistern house inside the second gatehouse. A boy of nine, Tam Roke's son, walked up at ten past three to tell him it had stopped, and said: *then it wasn't yours.*
- The municipal cistern rows for 7–12 are **empty**. The low pump has been dry since Day 9. The row's three tanks, about sixty pails and the yard cistern are full of the same crosscurrent-tainted water, and the district has to decide in the morning whether any of it can be used.
- The plate under the yard is **ticking every ten or twelve seconds**. Tarin could not tell whether that was iron cooling in the night or water finding a way round the bolts.
- A record stone beside the low pump carries *Glassstep low pump branch*, a date, and a crooked mark. Its other face is still blank, and Tarin cannot cut a line on it.
- Tarin is suspended since **Day 10** under **charge one — tampering with city records**, and was charged on **Day 20** under **charge two — unauthorized opening of a sealed structure and service of water from a reservoir the ledger records as closed.** Both open. Different subjects, different dates, not joined.
- The review is two days out and will slip. The cisterns ran out on Day 21. Do not resolve either.

## Batch goal

Show what it costs to be right. The Chapter 10 window did not make the route Tarin's; it bought a district one night of water and put a second charge on him. This batch's job is to take that apart: a district that has decided to be visible and is about to find out what visibility costs, a conservator whose evidence is about to be locked in a room she cannot enter, and a man who, with nothing left, forces a route alone and learns exactly what he is.

## Required character changes

- **Tarin** must fail. Chapter 17 is a real, physical, named failure, and it is expensive. He cannot tell afterwards whether he lost the route through the missing witness, the missing record, or his own standing, and he must not be told either.
- **Tarin / Mara** reach a **conditional agreement to share evidence** somewhere in this movement, on terms, while continuing to distrust each other's methods. It is a contract with conditions attached, not a reconciliation, and it is never repaired into trust. It happens here rather than at the climax.
- **Mara** must pay a career cost in Chapter 16 for handling Glassstep's originals in front of the residents without their consent, or for a worse version of that. Her hand-drawn key is still unlogged and still the only copy.
- **Hanne Roeg** is the one who decides to put a named household on paper. It must read as courage and as exposure at the same time, and **nobody may be let off the hook of it by being conveniently cowardly.**
- **Tam Roke** said no in Chapter 10 and was right to. He must not be reversed. His refusal is the template for the Chapter 39 refusal.
- **Niko** is twenty-two, impatient, and knows a descent that no survey records. A suspended engineer cannot authorise it and has no money to pay anybody who goes. The descent is set up here and paid for in Movement III.

## The three live pressures

None of these is a rerun of Batch 0001.

1. **Glassstep files for recognition.** The district asks the warden's office to enter it in the book. This is not a request for water; it is a request to be countable — a name, a ration row, a line on the district schedule. The petition must be signed by a named representative, which puts one household on paper where the Commissioner's office can find it. **The petition does not succeed in Volume 1.** It must be refused, and the refusal must be worse than a simple no.
2. **Mara's volume goes into restricted handling.** Her supervisor invokes the rule that an unverified record must never decide a public route, and the folio is locked away on a date she learns late. Her hand-drawn key is the only copy outside the restriction and she is the only person who can read it. Her problem is **durability, not nerve**: one person's memory of a document is not a record. She gets durability by a **legitimate** route — a second, independent copy of the same substitution pattern, taken from the Glassstep survey originals, which means going to Glassstep and handling paper in front of the people who live there.
3. **Tarin forces the branch without a witness, and it fails.** He has a gauged oak stop-log and eleven hours of arithmetic, and he has no witness and no standing. The plate under the yard weeps through a floor, the district's last dry tap runs brackish, and the follow-up Yara Sen cannot fund arrives on top of the three children she already has.

## Where the relationship lands

The Tarin–Mara agreement is the volume's relationship milestone and it happens in this movement, on conditions, and it is not a love scene and not a reconciliation. He gets her method. She gets field access she has no other way to obtain. Neither gets the other thing they want, and neither of them pretends otherwise.

## The turn

The petition is answered, and the answer is worse than a refusal: the warden's office will enter Glassstep in the book only on a route record — a named route, a documented source, a witness, and a date. No such record exists, and the only document that could supply part of one is the Ashgate leaf now inside the restricted stack. The district's legal existence and the volume's central document turn out to be the same object. The other route to the same answer is to go and look at the physical source, which is Niko's descent. **Movement III therefore begins from a choice, not a summons.**

## Hard mechanics — the prose must not contradict these

- **The head is 5·89 m** and it is a real pressure head. It has not changed and Tarin cannot increase it. Nothing in this batch makes more water appear.
- **The gauge log reads 5·89 in Office notation (5·89) and spoken aloud.** Record keepers write `5·89`; the engineer says "five point eight nine." Keep that split.
- **A channel stops at the balance point.** The row's branch stopped at 03:00 because the vessels reached the head in the branch, not because of anything anybody did. Any later attempt has to be reasoned from the same physics.
- **A gauged stop-log meters the branch; an ungauged one does not.** In Chapter 17 he has the oak, so the failure is not the flow rate. It is that a route with a name, a record, and a keeper can be held, and a route with only a man in it cannot. Say that in engineering terms, not in magic terms.
- **The eleven-hour window does not repeat, and no second window is won in this movement.** The number stays eleven because the source does not grow; if a new window is even discussed, nobody has earned it.
- **Two charges remain open and must not be merged, upgraded, or resolved.** A hearing may be mentioned. Do not hold it. It is not due until Movement V.
- **The Ashgate maintenance log runs about a hundred and eighty years** of a shut gate at 5·7 and a bit. The removed reading is the last line, the year before the flood.
- **The substituted leaf is twenty-six years old, not a hundred and eighty-one.** The reading on it is a hundred and eighty-one years old. Court-supply ink is issued in dated batches stamped on the tin lid, and the Office bought that batch twenty-six years ago and has never run out of it.
- **The Glassstep pump marker stone has two fixed faces.** The **old weathered face** carries *Glassstep low pump branch*, the date, the word of the day, and **under the date** Tam Roke's crooked mark. The **other face is blank and stays blank for the whole volume.** Chapter 10 has Tarin set the chisel on the blank face to put his own name there as the record of an act done without authority, and the Chapter 7 debt stops his hand. That blank face is the referent of the fixed Chapter 10 Gauge line, so **do not write the crooked mark on the blank face, and do not write the blank face as cut.** Niko argued for the blank face and Tarin overruled him on the page, in Chapter 6, so the choice is settled.
- **Tarin's Vellum trip is a day and a night of mountain road each way, not four days.** He leaves on Day 4, is in the provenance room on the morning of **Day 5**, writes his answer in the coach on the evening of Day 5, and comes down into the fold in the middle of the afternoon of Day 6. The Day 1 gate opening is **06:11** and he says *eleven minutes past six*; the Day 7 branch opening is at **ten past six**, which is a different event. Do not merge the two.
- **Every cistern figure in Batch 0001 was counted back from Day 21, and it has to stay that way.** The six fixed instances are twenty (Day 1 board), sixteen (Chapter 5), fourteen (Chapter 7), twelve (the Day 9 district return), eleven (Chapter 9), and "on the twenty-first there would be nothing in them at all" (Chapter 10). This batch starts **after** that deadline: the municipal cistern rows are empty and are not refilled. **Any new tank, row, or return figure must be counted forward from Day 21, and no municipal cistern supply is restored in Movement II** — only Glassstep's own branch water, the yard cistern, and whatever the movement earns. Two of these six figures were wrong in the first delivery because a figure was carried across a calendar change instead of re-derived; do not carry one again.
- **Nothing in this batch may be described as the reservoir, the Blind Reservoir, the One Map, or Boryn Vey.** Tarin has been up the hill four times and has found a gate, a head, a channel, and a plate. That is the whole of what he knows.
- **Roe is not threatened in this movement.** Nobody is currently asking who removed the pressure figure. His file on Tarin is finished and correct enough to hold; that is all he has to do.
