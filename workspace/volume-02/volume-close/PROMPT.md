## The corrected climax figures. Use these and no others

- **850 gallons an hour** is the launder's figure the Reach agreed in the second week of the month before and agreed to print, and it is a lath and a stop-watch in a slot two inches wide and an inch and a half deep. **844** is the launder's own figure from a section of six inches by about three-quarters of an inch, derived twice in Chapter 87. **They are not the same quantity and the twenty-five gallons between them are NOT resolved.** The window is **3,485**.
- **Four hours and six minutes is four point one hours. 850 × 4.1 = 3,485. 844 × 4.1 = 3,460.** **The window closed at six minutes past three in the morning and every closing time in the volume says six minutes past three.** An earlier draft said a quarter past three in five places, which is four hours fifteen minutes, and it was wrong.
- **3,485 is about one part in fifteen hundred of the Reach's own season of 5,481,000; about three quarters of one household's day at 4,725; about a fifth of one household's whole one-fifth share of 18,900; about a fifth of one day of the Sallow Dyke's 17,640; and 3.71, three and three-quarter gallons, a property across 940 properties.** All five are on the page and none is to be derived again.
- **NOT 861 AND NOT 3,540.** Both were wrong on the page in `outline/volume-02.md` and were corrected at the Batch 0002 review.
- **The volume's arithmetic against the window, which the climax says out loud on a coping and which the outline requires:** the Reach's assize at one fifth is 5,481,000; the window is 3,485; **the window prevents one field failure and does not prevent the Reach's season**; 850 an hour for ten days over 1,700 acres is a hundred and twenty gallons an acre, and a physician has said out loud that a hundred and twenty gallons an acre is not a crop; and the Sallow Dyke is putting 17,640 gallons a day out of the Reach's own lowest field into the Sere and therefore into Cresset, which is three hundred and ten days of the Reach's own season a year, for six years, and it cannot be used and cannot be stopped.
