## The five jobs

1. **Cresset and the Reach find each other in one document, and the document is the fourth copy of a card with a hole in it.** A ration review in a city on a ration cut by a fifth, in papers, finds a district's supply entered on a return as the works at Sallow Head, and the works are in a valley sixty-one miles west that has no office, no row, no schedule entry and no keeper, **and the water going into the Sere out of a clay in the Reach's lowest field is the same water.** **The document must be ordinary, must be a form, and must be readable by anybody.** And neither side is a villain: Cresset is a city on a cut of a fifth that has been given a supply it did not have, and the Reach is a valley that has been losing water for six years. **The collision is a paper collision, and both parties are behaving correctly.**
2. **The amendment of six years ago is read in a room with a hundred and forty people in it, by Margit Saye, who has come back.** **She is the only person alive who can read the hand. She refused three times in three rooms and she has not been thanked once. And she has said the method is a desk and not a man, and the Guild keeps no withdrawn specimen, and the Guild's own good rule is the reason the question cannot be answered.** A hundred and forty people want a name. **She does not give one, and the reason she gives is the strongest thing anybody says in the batch, and what the room does with the answer is the batch's politics.**
3. **The cost of a service window is priced in gallons a household a day, and nobody can be made to accept it.** The Reach's own assize at one fifth is 4,725 a household a day. **The Reach counts in gallons a household a day and Cresset counts in gallons a head a day and there is no instrument in this basin that converts one into the other without a census.** That was stated in Chapter 59 and set aside and it must now be paid. **A service window is 844 gallons an hour for four hours and six minutes, which is 3,485 gallons, which is three hundred and ten days of the Sallow Dyke, and which is a fifteenth of one household's day, and every one of those four comparisons is checkable.** **The window has to be offered to somebody and somebody has to be told the price, and the person told must not be able to refuse, and the chapter must not let anybody off the hook.**
4. **Iven Marrow answers for six years, in writing, in his own hand, at the end of Movement IV, and it costs him.** **HE HAS ALREADY DONE IT ONCE. His letter of one paragraph, which came down on the fortnight cart on the seventeenth of this month, addressed to Perdie Ammid, unasked for, answering nothing, saying he wrote the sixth entry in the shed card and has had a shelf in a counting-house for eleven years and that he would like to have written the seventh, and that he has written to the man at Ostre and had no answer either, and that he has had a great deal of reason in nine days to make a sentence about the sluice and has not made it, and would like it known that he did not make it and not that he could not, and ending *the bench is on a Tuesday and nothing I have said in this letter is part of the offer*.** **MOVEMENT IV'S LETTER IS THE SECOND ONE AND IT IS A DIFFERENT THING AND NOT A REPETITION.** The first was a man declining to make a sentence. The second is a man making one, in the same hand, about six years, on a subject he is not going to be thanked for, and it is the best thing he says in the series so far and **it costs him his standing in his own company**, and the chapter must show the cost on the page and must not soften it and must not let anybody rescue him. **He gains nothing. He is not in the room when anything is decided against him. He is not caught out. He does not use magic. He does not know the name of anything. His price is unchanged and the offer does not expire.**
5. **Movement IV ends with a cost in two places and not with a win.** The two places are **Cresset and the Reach**, and the cost is that Cresset is a city on a cut of a fifth that has just been shown a supply it does not have and cannot refuse, and the Reach is a valley that has just been shown a party it cannot refuse. **And the one thing this batch must not do is make anybody a villain.** The clerk in Cresset is doing his job.
