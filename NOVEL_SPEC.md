# Novel Specification

Title: The Irrigation Ghost
Repository slug: novel-irrigation-ghost
Genre: water fantasy / regional mystery
Male lead: A water engineer who moves water through remembered places
Length target: 600 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A drought-struck city discovers that its vanished reservoirs are connected to a ghost in the municipal records.

## Ending
Water is restored without forcing people to forget who they were.
