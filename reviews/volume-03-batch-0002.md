## The blocker

**Chapter 0113 had Tarin in Vellum.** The closing scene at the plank had him answer the boy with *I was at Vellum, on a table, with four questions on a page*. The volume's tenth prohibition is that he cannot leave a valley, the only mileage in the batch is sixty-one miles east and two days of road each way, and Chapter 0120 closes on *He could not leave*. Either a continuity break or an unmarked lie told to a boy, and neither is admissible. Three state files asserted the opposite of the chapter.

**Fixed whole.** He is at the plank all day — the noon trial, the four, and the bank since half past four — and Perdie Ammid is the one who went down the road on the Monday and came back on the Thursday. What she tells him at the bottom of the bank is the whole of the session in her own words, in about a hundred words. Three state files corrected.

## The eight majors

1. **"Thursday the seventh" three times; the seventh is a Saturday.** Now a Saturday in 0111 twice, 0114 once, and the coping stone and the district book follow the page. Cobb's promise of the works sum moved from *on Thursday* to *on the Monday after they are done*.
2. **The month turn put a week away, four times, when the month after next begins Day 187.** Now: Harl Bray is not back before the month turns, which is the month after next; Mrs Bray has a month of him not here; and he comes home on the Wednesday the eleventh, three days short of a run, because Jess Marr put a boy on the flour boat on the Monday with a folded paper carrying a door number and not his name. The count reaching out of the valley is now a thing that happens.
3. **Chapter 0114 re-printed in a shop the sentence Dorrin struck out in the lane two days earlier.** Perdie now reads the finding as it stands and says, without looking up, that a fourth clause was struck in the lane and that the striking is on the record.
4. **The materials figure was given twice and the two could not both be true** (about eleven shillings in 0115, four shillings and eightpence in 0117, and the eleven collided with the labour). 0115 now prices nothing and states only what was carried.
5. **A leather washer was charged to the seventh in a bill form and cut out of a boot on the eighth.** Removed from the form.
6. **"Ten names given" was one more than the doors yield.** Now nine names given, one withheld by the person and recorded unwilling, one person absent and not named for him because his mother would not be asked. The figure agrees in Nye's return line, Harker's note and the district-book entry, and the machine's inability to name an absent man is a second thing the row learned that morning.
7. **The bank view called four persons "of the row" when one is a man of Nether Sallow.** The finding now names three of the row and one of Nether Sallow in the chapter and in the Friday sheet, Dorrin says so, and Corrie Lille notices on the Monday that the row is being charged for the man from across the ford and Cobb says it is, and that he thought about it on the water. The sheet is still true and it is still charging the row.
8. **Chapter 0112's ending was stitched out of order** — the butt full at the fortieth and then the count restarting at thirty-five, same beat twice. The reflection now falls at about the thirtieth trip and the last ten are carried without counting.

## The fact-changing minors, all fixed

*the volume* is gone from Tarin's sworn evidence and reads *the book*. *the reader of all three* is gone from sheet 39. A pot went onto a shop hob in 0119 and came straight off it, so the batch has no tea. Mrs Halward is about fifty and forty-one years in the valley, not seventy-one (Aldis Kerr's age, from Chapter 0101). Nell Trew is the girl of the fourth door and not Sella Marr's granddaughter, and Sella now says *my daughter's boy*, which fixes a relationship the batch had been floating. The store book's entry carries a month and a year at the head of its page, so the fortnight inference is supported by the evidence quoted. The form has nine boxes, not eleven. Nine loads, not eleven shillings. Thirty-eight trips behind him, not forty-one. Harker had two days, not nine. Cobb does not invent a rate on a Sunday in a Monday chapter. About nine people came out of five doors, not eleven. The boy of fifteen's provenance claim is corrected by Tarin, at length, in the one passage that polices provenance. Chapter 0120's Harl Bray material is rebuilt around the message from Jess.

## Inherited, recorded, not re-cut

**Chapters 0101, 0106 and 0116 date the removal of the notice from the frame to the Tuesday of the second of this month, and the second of that month was a Friday.** Three delivered chapters and a state file carry it. On this project's own rule, a delivered chapter's weekday was not re-cut, and **Chapter 0120 now says *the evening of the second of this month* and names no weekday**, so the error is neither multiplied nor corrected. It is recorded here once.

## One finding the review got wrong, recorded so it is not re-raised

**Chapter 0118 says the reading of the thirty-first was *ten days ago*, in a chapter dated the tenth.** Day 155 to Day 165 is ten days. It is ten, it is correct, and no chapter may change it to eleven.

**One finding the repair pass did not accept, and it is a judgement worth recording.** The review said Job 3 was only partially delivered because on the page Tarin's hand does nothing in either the making-good or the pump-and-well day — he is given a hod and told to carry it, and Sella tells him a pump is a machine. The batch substitutes a body among bodies for a hand among hands, and says so. **The repair pass added the beat the card asked for: in Chapter 0115 Cobb asks him where the foot of the bank wants to go, he answers in figures, Wick Venn takes a bar and checks it, the check agrees, and Cobb enters it in the day-book as *the check being the authority and not the saying*.** His hand is useful because a man with a bar agreed with it in front of six people, which is the social condition and not an advance.
