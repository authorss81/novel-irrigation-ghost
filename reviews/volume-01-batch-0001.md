## Pass 2 — verification and repair

### Blocking

**B1. Chapter 7 broke a hard terminology rule.** The upper-markets name was printed as *memory bleed*. `bible/terminology.md` says the name is *the scaling* and that it is *never* called a bleed, that root being reserved for community bleed and name debt; `outline/series.md` also uses "a memory bleed" for an unrelated Volume 6 phenomenon. Corrected to *the scaling*.

**B2. The marker stone's face-state was described four incompatible ways, and two of them falsified the climax's fixed Gauge line.** Chapter 6 had Niko promising the blank face as the one "left for the next thing," had Tarin cut the weathered face anyway without answering him, and had "turned the stone back" for the mark; Chapter 10 had name, date and mark on one face with the other blank, and then twice more said the mark was on "the other side," one of those sentences self-contradictory as written. The line *The stone is blank. Two hands are on it* only has a referent under the first Chapter 10 description.

Fixed without cutting anything: the **old weathered face** now carries the route name, the date, and Roke's crooked mark under the date; the **other face is blank and stays blank for the volume.** Niko's advice is now overruled by Tarin on the page, with a reason, which converts an ignored setup into a short exchange about what a record is for. Chapter 6's "turned the stone back" and Chapter 10's two "other side" references are corrected, and Chapter 10 now says which face was cut. The state is written into `state/continuity.md`.
