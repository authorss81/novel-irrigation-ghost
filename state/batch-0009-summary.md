## The arithmetic, all of it derived on the page and all of it checkable

- **844 gallons an hour, from a section and not from a lath, and derived twice.** The flume is a six-inch stone launder and runs about three-quarters of an inch deep. **6 in × ¾ in = 4½ square inches.** 144 ÷ 4½ = **32 exactly**, so it is a thirty-second of a square foot; at about a foot a second that is a thirty-second of a cubic foot a second; a cubic foot is 7½ gallons, the only figure in this basin that has never once been wrong; 7.5 ÷ 32 = 0.234375 gal/s = **843.75, called 844.**
- **Four hours and six minutes is four point one hours.** **844 × 4.1 = 3,460. 850 × 4.1 = 3,485.** The twenty-five gallons between them are left on the page and are **not resolved**: 850 is a lath and a stop-watch in a slot two inches wide and an inch and a half deep, 844 is a section of launder at an assumed velocity, **and they are not the same quantity.** The Reach works to 850 because that is the figure it agreed and agreed to print, and a fitter's arithmetic in a room in a drought does not overrule a village's own figure. **This is the 850/190 reconciliation's third instance and it is NOT the same as the 660/667 reconciliation, which is shut and was not re-derived.**
- **3,485 against four things, all checked on the page.** Against the Reach's own season of 5,481,000: **about one part in fifteen hundred** (5,481,000 ÷ 3,485 = 1,572.7). Against one household's day at one fifth, 4,725: **about three quarters of a household's day** (0.74). Against one household's whole one-fifth share, 18,900: **about a fifth** (18,900 ÷ 3,485 = 5.42). Against the Sallow Dyke's 17,640 a day: **about a fifth of one day of it** (17,640 ÷ 3,485 = 5.06).
- **The comparison that cannot be made is the whole of the chapter.** The Reach counts in gallons a household a day; Cresset counts in gallons a head a day; there is no instrument in this basin that converts one into the other and there has not been one since the assize, which was done by men counting chimneys. **Chapter 59 stated this and set it aside.
