# Open Threads

These are bootstrap promises, not unresolved chapter events. Each should advance through the volume noted without replacing a local promise with the larger mystery.

1. **Ashgate and the first Gauge:** The missing reservoir is physically present below a later settlement; the opening stream is residual pressure, not created water. Pay off the immediate water crisis in Volume 1 while leaving the larger route mystery open.
2. **The collective ghost:** The Irrigation Ghost is the accumulated pressure of unlisted places and people, not Lira alone. Hold the many-voiced reading until **Chapter 24**. A single voice may be heard earlier, but the crowd must not be identifiable as a crowd before the descent, or the reveal arrives fourteen chapters early. Answer its identity by Volume 7 without a resurrection.
3. **The One Map and Blind Reservoir:** The system uses address plates, pressure relief, and rain shutters to make water unreachable. Reveal the physical mechanism progressively; the final answer remains the planned monopoly, not a new entity.
4. **Boryn's hidden chronology:** Boryn reactivated the newest protocol six years before the story and expanded it afterward. Introduce his public role in Volume 5 and make the earlier, smaller operation part of his credible motive.
5. **Glassstep and Lira:** Tarin's childhood route, mother's choices, and the unlisted neighborhood are a personal consequence of the system. Do not turn the quest into finding a returned mother.
6. **Tarin and Mara:** Their relationship must pass through professional suspicion, public disagreement, consent boundaries, and shared institutional work before any romantic resolution.
7. **Local readers and other routes:** Ensure at least one major crisis is solved by another community's reader or keeper. Tarin must never be the only person who can make water move.
8. **Ending constraints:** Track the physical cost of every route operation, the source of every restored water supply, and whether consent is real. The final restoration must not require anyone to be compelled to forget.
9. **Glassstep's petition and the physical source:** **The petition was filed on Day 22 and returned on Day 34.** It fails: the warden's office will enter the district only on a route record — named route, documented source, witness, date — and the only document that could supply the source is the restricted Ashgate folio, so the district's legal existence and the volume's central document are the same object. **The two routes to the same answer are the scheduled reading day (the eleventh of the next month, a Friday) and Niko's descent.** Movement III starts from that choice. Do not let the petition succeed in Volume 1: the reservoir is acknowledged, the district is not, and that difference is the point the ending will need.

- How will communities use the freedom to disagree after the Many Rivers Charter is adopted?
- What is under the plate. It is a lid on the terrace overflow shaft, the shaft is full, and the ground above it came up on Day 30. It is not the reservoir and has not been revealed as one.

## Carried to Volume 2

- **The second voice.** The Gauge spoke once in Chapter 5 and once in Chapter 10, plainly, each time with a second pressure underneath that is neither explanation nor advice. Both were written as a **pressure**, not as a sound, and each used a different set of physical images. **No Gauge panel appears in Chapters 11–20 at all**, so the thread is untouched and intact. **Chapters 1–23 must not confirm it, must not name it, and must not let a character say it aloud.** Do not re-use a crowd image for it; the second time it was written as a room and a rope, not as people. The many-voiced reading itself happens in Chapter 24, which is inside Volume 1.
- **Why the Guild's dry gate and the Reach's wet channel disagree.** Volume 1's final chapter image and the new-volume question both sit on this; the contradiction is upstream and must survive the first volume intact.
- **What is behind the plated terrace outfall.** Named as a work fact in Chapter 2, shown in Chapter 3, loaded in Chapters 6 and 10, audible in Chapter 10 as a ticking every ten or twelve seconds, and **answered in Chapter 18**: the lane has been a closed drainage system for thirty years, the ground is a saturated sponge, and the water table came up to the surface of the street on Day 30. The ticking was water round the bolts. The shaft below the plate is full and has been since before the register. Not the reservoir.
- **Who is pumping under the Ashgate works.** Seeded in Chapter 6, named as a fact in Chapter 9, and **given a rate in Chapter 19: two a second on the sixth, three a second by the twenty-ninth, without variation, and **stopping dead on the night of the 2nd of the next month, in the middle of Niko's hand on a coil of somebody else's rope.** A pump making up for a head it has lost is not a mystery. Nobody in Movement II asks who owns it, and the certificate Mara Quill files says *no further action is proposed at present* for a year. The descent goes into that filter house from the quarry side, in a cut no survey records, and a man was on a staging twenty feet down on the 2nd of the next month.
- **Why a true reading survives a record that was built to discredit it.** The leaf was cut out and replaced, and the only surviving copy of the reading is a pressure shadow that nobody intended to preserve. Nobody has asked why. **Someone needed that number to stay where it could be found.**
- **Wend Quill.** The hand on both redactions and Mara's mother's brother. **Chapter 16 moved this:** Mara wrote to her mother, and the certificate she filed describes the hand in terms only one person in the commissioner's registry could read. The registry's marginal note says no further action is proposed at present and is dated a Tuesday in the second month of next year, and nothing whatever happens on it. He remains evidence of an office, not a villain, and is not the reason Roe's lie falls.
- **Niko's descent.** Named in Chapter 10, set up in Chapters 14, 17 and 19, and **partly walked in Chapter 19**: the mouth of the cut, fresh spoil, four prints in and one set out, a lit lantern, an open tin of oil, a coil of rope cut by somebody else's knife, and a man on a staging. **The going down past that staging is unspent and is Movement III's threshold beat.** Movement III pays for it. Niko has no money, no authority, no crew, and a debt at a chandler's.
- **The hundred and four names.** Mara read them aloud in a yard on Day 28 and stopped twice, at the eleventh and the fourteenth, both times because the household identified itself. She has not transcribed either. The certificate records the identifications as events and not as evidence, and the fourth step is now a custodian of a record in its own hand. This is the institutional form the series generalises, and it is intact.
- **Mara Quill's unlogged drawing.** Still unlogged, still in her drawer under a tray of brass weights, and still the only copy that nobody can check. She did not destroy it and she did not enter it. Condition two of the Chapter 16 agreement exists because of it.

These are living-world uncertainties, not missing central-mystery answers.
