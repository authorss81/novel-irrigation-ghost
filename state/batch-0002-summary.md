## What the batch proves

1. **Being right is not the same as being able to act.** Tarin Rusk is right about the vessel, the head and the balance point on Day 30 and the ground comes up through a floor anyway, because the thing that could have gone wrong was not the thing he was standing in front of.
2. **A name is a physical constraint, not a formality.** The eleven hours of water in Chapter 10 could not be spent, could not be given away, and could not be sold, because a district with no row cannot be given to anybody. Every route to water in this movement runs through a piece of paper, and the only piece of paper that would move is the one locked in a room.
3. **Visibility has a price and the people who pay it are named.** Glassstep chose to be countable on Day 22 and paid for it in a registry, a barrow, an £86 that cannot be spent on a child, a room with a wet floor, a closed tap, a doctor and a form due inside thirty days. Nobody was let off the hook of that choice by being conveniently frightened.
4. **The relationship milestone belongs here.** Tarin and Mara agree on five conditions on a dispensary step, in front of nine people, and each gets the method they need and not the thing they want, and they go on distrusting each other's methods.
5. **The mystery does not advance, and the pressure does.** Nobody asks who removed the pressure figure. A registrar writes *no further action is proposed at present* and dates it a year out. A pump under the terrace gets faster and nobody names it.

## New canon recorded in this batch

- **The plate answered.** It is a lid on the terrace overflow shaft, the shaft below it is full, and the district has been a closed drainage system for thirty years. The ticking every ten or twelve seconds was water finding its way round the bolts. The lane's ground is a saturated sponge and its water table came up to the surface of the street on Day 30. Not the reservoir.
- **The missing second hand is the other end of the pipe.** A route with a name, a record and a keeper can be held; a route with only a man in it cannot be shut, and the man who shuts a route is the man at the wheel on the works at four in the morning. This is the load-bearing discovery of Chapter 14 and it is what makes the Chapter 17 failure inevitable without making it a mystery.
- **The run's own flow has never been measured by anyone, for one physical reason:** there has never been anywhere for it to go. Tarin had the only number that bore on it — the run's note dropped by a third when the Chapter 10 log went in — in his book for ten days, and did the Day 30 sum without it. That is the compounding error, and it is named on the page.
- **Glassstep's own count: 24 houses, 126 persons**, taken by the district on a slate over a day and a half, with two people entered at the margin as declining enumeration and 104 people never asked.
- **Yara Sen's requirement is 70 gallons a day of clean water**; Bel Crale's dispensary could only ever afford 70 a week off a cart at twopence, which is the standing reason four children are ill. On Day 31 she holds £7 16s, which buys 22 gallons.
- **The district sold 680 gallons of its own cistern for £86** between the 28th and the 31st, at 1d rising to 3d, and the price of clean water went from 2d to 8d in three days. It now holds more money than it has had since the flood year and cannot spend a penny of it on a child.
- **Hanne Roeg is 71** and is a **custodian of a record in her own hand** — the second of the two one-to-one tracings of the Glassstep redaction folio, made in a yard, from one original, in one sitting, by two named hands. This is the institutional form the whole series generalises, and it arrives eleven chapters before anyone expects it.
- **A partial witness is a category.** Old Marden asked for his name to be cut in two and got it.
- **A witness must be able to say no and must be able to answer for ten years.** Tarin refuses the boy of nine out loud, in front of his mother, and writes the rule down.
- **Pell's institution bites.** He filed the first city document containing the words *the blue steps*, and it says there is no row, no keeper and no schedule entry. He closed the district's last municipal tap on a condition entry.
- **Mara Quill's certificate is filed on the 4th of the next month** and produces a registry marginal note dated a year out saying nothing will happen on it.
- Minor cast added: **Marda Ollan**, 31, who keeps the keys to the top end; **the Tall boy**; a district-return runner; **Hallam**, named by a voice under a quarry; and the unnamed contractor man on a staging. The boy of nine remains unnamed.
- `state/continuity.md` now carries every Batch 0002 number in one place, including the derived evaporation figure and the two prices per gallon, with an instruction to re-derive rather than carry.

## Repairs applied after the verification pass
