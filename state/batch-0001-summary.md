## What the batch proves

1. **The drought is a lie about infrastructure, not a natural failure.** A head of 5·89 m has been standing behind a gate the city has recorded as shut and dry since before the flood, and the head survives in the pressure shadow of a page somebody cut out of a book.
2. **The power is real and cannot create water.** Every drop in these ten chapters traces to a head, a gate, a branch, a plate, a cistern, a person, or the one measurable rain of the opening. Nothing arrives for free.
3. **Tarin's competence is what makes the first miracle survivable.** He is Stage 0 for four chapters and is good at his job on the page: float gauges, stilling tubes, stop-gates, head loss, gauging a stop-log, and reading a warden's board.
4. **The first cost is specific and permanent.** The two-finger press he has used since he was thirteen, and the taste of his own tea. Neither returns in this batch, and the loss is what forces the Chapter 10 climax to be spoken rather than cut.

## New canon recorded in this batch

- **Wend Quill**, Mara's mother's brother, is the hand on both redactions. Nineteen when he did the Glasscopy work at their mother's table, still in the acting commissioner's registry twenty-six years later. He is evidence of a pattern, not a mastermind, and the pattern belongs to an office.
- **Court-supply ink is issued in dated batches stamped on the tin lid.** The Office has never run out of one tin. This is how a twenty-six-year-old batch was still in use within the last six years.
- **The Ashgate pressure log runs about a hundred and eighty years** of a shut gate with a head of 5·7 and a bit, never varying.
- **The record stones of a sealed works are carted to Vellum** and left on a *pending disposal* slip. Folio eleven's stone is cracked from the cart and has its name cut twice, out of register.
- **Sluice wardens record the time a condition begins**, not the time they arrive. The blank right-hand column in Pell's entry book is the whole of Chapter 10's clock.
- **A gauged stop-log meters a branch.** The oak Roke planed is set to a pin hole; it is what makes eleven hours an engineering figure rather than a magical one.
- **A channel stops at the balance point**, where the water in the receiving vessels is level with the head in the branch. This is why the courtyard cistern held at a third on Day 1 and why the Chapter 10 branch stopped by itself at 03:00.
- **Salt fever has local names:** *salt fever* in the Reach, *the scaling* in the upper markets, and nothing in office language. (The regional synonym deliberately avoids the word *bleed*, which `bible/terminology.md` reserves for community bleed and name debt.)
- Minor cast: Sluice Warden **Pell**; **Hanne Roeg**, **Wick**, **Bel Crale** and **Tam Roke** in Glassstep; **Deryn Fenn** and **Osla Vint** at the Water Office; **Hollis Kreft**, Mara's reading supervisor; the old woman named the district in Chapter 6 is Hanne Roeg, and the boy at the gatehouse at 03:00 is Tam Roke's son, nine.
