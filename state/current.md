## WHAT MOVEMENT III DID, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**A paper road was built and torn up in the same month.** On a printed Guild form a hundred and thirty years old, a man of thirty-three wrote four true lines saying the Sallow Reach has water and supply district 14, North Sallow has none — every figure true, and not one of them about the same reach — and left the fifth line empty, because the fifth line is the Sere's third sentence in types and it wants a person at a door, and he cannot be one.
