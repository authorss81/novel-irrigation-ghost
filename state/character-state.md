# Character State

Updated after Batch 0001 (Volume 01, Chapters 1–10, Days 1–20). Compact and factual; use this for the next batch rather than re-reading the chapters.

## Tarin Rusk — protagonist

- **Age 33.** Senior flow engineer, Cresset Water Office, four years in the lower terrace district.
- **Power:** Stage 2 (Name), reached deliberately in Chapter 6 and repeated once in Chapter 10. Stage 1 (Trace) was first reached in Chapter 5, perception only. He is at the **beginning** of Name. He has no method for finding a witness and cannot repeat an opening on his own.
- **Status:** Suspended since **Day 10** under **charge one — tampering with city records**. Charged again on **Day 20** under **charge two — unauthorized opening of a sealed structure and service of water from a reservoir the ledger records as closed.** Two open charges, two subjects, two dates.
- **Lost:** desk, seal, district reference, keys to the lower terrace works, access to the lower terrace, and his post. He keeps the blue waxed field book, the bar, the folding rule, the chalk stub, and the tin pot.
- **Name debt (minor, Chapter 7):** the two-finger press — the tap he has used since he was thirteen on spindles, floats, and page edges. Also the taste of his own bitter tea. **Neither returns in this batch** and neither may return in Chapter 10; he cannot re-cut a record stone. Volume 2's debt is a different thing (a professional association, not a second lost hand technique).
- **What he has learned to do and cannot yet repeat:** naming requires a trace, a willing witness, and a record he cuts himself; it does *not* require legitimacy, and without it the receiving side takes the crosscurrent as salt fever. He has stated this to Yara Sen and understands it.
- **He has refused twice on the page:** his own private memory of the Glassstep lane as an anchor (Chapter 6), and — established as a pattern, not a surprise — he will refuse Mara's in Chapter 39.
- **He has never used anyone's private memory as an anchor.** Do not write him reaching for one without a chapter that pays for it.
- **Open wants:** to answer the charges; to find out where the water comes from (he has said aloud in a crowd that he does not know); to keep the district out of a classification that erases it.
- **His stated fear, in his own words, Chapter 10:** that being useful enough is what lets him decide for people who never would have accepted it.

## Mara Quill — the slow-burn relationship

- **Age 31.** Provenance record conservator, Municipal Memory Office, Vellum. Eleven years in service. Non-magical and stays non-magical for the series.
- **Power:** none, and never any. Her expertise is provenance — nib cut, gum, batch stamp on the tin lid, sizing, watermark, pressure shadow, pressure of the stroke.
- **Wants:** Glassstep's legal name, land claims, and basin share. She put her own origin on the page in her own point of view in Chapter 8 and has told nobody in dialogue: born in the second house on a lane in a district that is on no sheet she has ever certified, to a mother who kept the pump books for a household of canal workers, eleven years in a building that has never once had her mother's lane to hand.
- **Holds:** a hand-drawn one-to-one key of the substitution pattern, drawn in her own ink, **not entered in the hand-drawing register**. It is the only copy outside the restriction and she is the only person who can read it. She chose that on purpose, on condition that nobody can check her.
- **Vulnerability:** the folio went into restricted handling on Day 3. The restriction decision she is waiting on has no date she knows. She has said nothing to Tarin, and Tarin has not asked.
- **Relationship position:** professional respect reached in Chapter 5 and neither of them wanted it. They have not corresponded directly; her two-line answer of Day 15 was written above her and she let him know that. **She is the only character who has heard the word *Gauge* spoken**, in Chapter 5, and her position on it is on the page: she has never in her life been told anything by a piece of paper. **No agreement to share evidence yet** — that belongs to Chapters 11–20, with conditions attached, and it is never repaired into trust.
- **Family complication:** the hand on both redactions is her mother's brother, **Wend Quill**, still in the acting commissioner's registry. She has not told Tarin and has not decided what to do.

## Sluice Warden Pell

- Counting channels, not stories. Will record a condition; will not record a story; will not invent a row.
- Holds the notice of Day 1 with the empty cause box, and the Day 20 entry with the timed right-hand column at 16:00.
- Has no sheet for the district, so the machinery of his office turned once and produced nothing. He is the man who said a warden has a sheet, and a clock, and a crowd.
- Now has filed **charge two** on his own dated and timed entry. He told Tarin he thinks what is on the board and nothing else.

## Halden Roe — the volume's antagonist

- Acting water commissioner, about fifty, temporary appointment, trying to make it permanent. Gracious, exact, and better at arguing than Tarin.
- **His falsification is arithmetic, and it is his own work over the last six years:** decommissioning certificates and a gauge certificate saying no residual head at Ashgate, filed so that a reservoir every ledger calls dead has no lawful allocation and therefore cannot be pumped from. A pumping plant in the old filter house has been running at night for the better part of a year.
- **He never uses magic and never learns to. He does not know the One Map's name and is not its architect.** Do not hint at anything larger behind him.
- Offered Tarin North Sallow pumping district and was refused. He has won the first exchange and does not yet know what he is standing next to.

## Yara Sen

- 41. Water physician and public-health examiner. Has been coming to Glassstep since the spring, telling the row their water is bad and the city lies, and has never been able to file a case, because the row has no name.
- Holds a written account of salt fever in four children caused by a route opened without a legitimacy condition, and follow-up she cannot fund.
- Refuses to be Tarin's ally, refuses to let him keep a branch running past a fever curve, and made him tell her the cause before she proved it. She does not stop treating on his account.
- She has heard the word *reader* and has no use for it. She told Tarin in Chapter 7 that the old accounts of readers working are worthless, and she does not treat any of it as evidence of anything. She is not a reader's ally and must not be written as one.

## Niko Venn

- 22. Apprentice, eleven months. Wants a formal place in the Water Office without pretending to a master's training.
- Knows the terrace by body, not by document, and beats Tarin at it. Refused, out loud, to be Tarin's reason for a route.
- **Now holds a fact nobody has asked him for: a descent into the old filter house off the quarry side, in a cut no survey records, which his father used to run water in a wet year.** Tarin wrote it in the blue book and underlined it. Movement II sets up the descent; Movement III pays for it.
- In the Chapter 10 yard, at the stop-gate, with a gauging stick, told not to speak.
