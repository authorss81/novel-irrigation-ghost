- **The weep, 2 gallons twice.** 2 gal in **1 min 20 s** = **90 gallons an hour**; 2 gal in **1 min 21 s** = 88.9, written **89**. Both printed. - **The plank on the fourteenth: 193 and 191, again**, off six gallons in 1 min 52 s and 1 min 53 s, and the figure is 190, and **Nesta Sarg says out loud that the same two numbers to the second, three weeks on, is a count that is worth more than one that comes out different, and that a different figure would have gone for first.**
- **The flume launder.** Six inches wide, about three-quarters of an inch deep, **four and a half square inches, which is a thirty-second of a square foot**, and a cubic foot is seven and a half gallons, and at exactly a foot a second that is **eight hundred and forty-four gallons an hour.** Jory Vant times it at twenty feet in twenty seconds, which is exactly a foot a second, and Margit Saye says a stone launder does not go at exactly a foot a second and uses the figure anyway, **because a Reach that lets a fitter substitute her own figure is a Reach that has two figures, and that is where this whole business started.**
- **The transit, which was unstated in the delivered batch and is now on the page.** The flume is two miles and four chains, which at a foot a second is very nearly three hours, and the water came off the last stone **twenty-six minutes** after the gate came up. **Margit Saye rules on the page that this is the front of the water and not the rate: a launder dry for six years on a fall of one in two hundred starts at the speed of a thing that has been let go, and the rate is the only one of the two a card can carry.** The front is not a figure anybody can use and is not printed on anything.
- **The window: 850 × 1 hour 40 minutes = 1,417 gallons.** Done out loud on the flags outside the shed, and the hundred minutes are counted **from the water's arrival at the outfall at half past eight to the chain going on at ten past ten**, which is why *the fortieth minute of the second hour* is literally true. **The sum uses 850 and not 844, and the reason is given on the page.**
