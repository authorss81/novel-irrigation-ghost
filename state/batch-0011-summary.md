## WHAT THIS BATCH IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**The water freed Thursday night goes down the long drain and stands in a lane on the far bank where a return has said *no inhabitants* for nineteen years, and a woman nineteen years in the third door is standing in it.** A washer is fitted, bread is carried, a certificate with a name in a box makes six doors countable, a Fair Copy magistrate seals two doors to protect a legal boundary and the bank gives way under the seal, an old flood book in a record house shows its last pages cut out with a pencil line fourteen years old and no name, a man admits a fourteen-year closure on a numbered sheet without giving names, a bank is held Sunday by four persons with a rope and a willow at their own request, and a flood court sits Monday and lays *persons accounted for* — eleven — where nineteen years said nobody. **And every evening a man fills a butt at a plank, forty trips, about an hour and sixteen minutes, and he cannot leave.**

## The second repair pass, and the external review that caused it

**An external review was run on the repaired batch and returned twelve blockers and nineteen smaller findings. Ten of the blockers were real and are fixed. The whole chapter-level record is at the foot of the Volume 03 block in `state/chapter-summaries.md`; this is the short version.**

**The three that would have propagated into Volume 04.** **The valley's sheets could not travel three incompatible ways and did not:** they were written on the Saturday the thirty-first, sent on Saturday night with a carter who rode through, and read at Vellum on the Sunday — and **Iven Marrow's letter, which is four days from Bexhill, no longer claims to have read them**; it claims a Fair Copy certificate that came to him on the Friday, which is possible. **Iven's one appearance in his own hand survives, at five lines, untalked of.** **Tarin's tank figure was a quarter of an inch and two sentences called it half an inch** — 2 ft 6⅛ down to 2 ft 5⅞ is a quarter — and it is now a quarter in Chapter 0103 and in every state file that said half. **The tally office's arithmetic did not add up:** four years of her own plus six of Hanne Ilk's is ten of the nineteen, and the remaining nine are in five unnamed hands, which makes **nineteen returns in seven hands**, and Mara's own count in the store now matches her count of the hands.

**The rest, in one line each.** **A quotation mark closed early in Chapter 0106 and forty-five words of a magistrate's speech ran unquoted; it is fixed.** **A copy of a shed day-book could not have come down with a declaration signed two days before the night it describes; it now comes down on the Wednesday with the district return, which is after the entry and before the sealing.** **Iven Marrow's *got a habit* sentence never reached Vellum — it was in a letter read aloud in a shop at Brant on the thirteenth — so Mara no longer quotes it, and the principle is now her own.** **The far-bank water arrived on the night of the twenty-second into the twenty-third, and the standing count is now seven nights by the twenty-ninth, eight days by the thirtieth and ten days by the first of next month, consistently, on the inclusive basis.** **Wick was twenty trips into a butt at noon and again at four; the butt is now filled from six, and Wick waits on the bank at four and talks at the wall during the filling.** **Chapter 0109 had fourteen years and eleven years for the same silence, and eleven had no referent; it is fourteen throughout.** **The launder is two miles and four chains from its own outfall, not four hundred yards** — the four hundred yards is the tank to the sluice — and the sluice is what is chained, not the launder.

**Three things the review caught that were dropped threads rather than errors, and that are now beats.** **Nesta Sarg promised on the Tuesday to tell Tarin to his face at six o'clock whether his hand was right, and nobody ever did it; Chapter 0106 now has Wick's Thursday stick reading on the shutter, taken against the same third doorstep he marked on the Tuesday morning, and Nesta keeping her word at the fourth trip, and Tarin a day and a half out** (third repair pass: the promise is now for Thursday, which is the day the stick reading is actually taken on, and the "half a day" is a day and a half). **The tank card was promised to be sealed on the Sunday and never was; the promise is gone.** **The Monday-morning arrangement Nesta made in the last third of Chapter 0101 — she does the morning, he does the evening — recurs in Chapter 0103.** And **Sella Marr cannot be standing in the doorway of a door she was ordered out of on the Friday, so in Chapter 0109 she is in the doorway of the fourth door with her own door still barred at the far end of the lane.**

**And one duplication the first pass missed:** **Chapter 0110 carried three consecutive state catalogues**, sixteen lines apart, in the chapter a reader will remember. **Two are gone and the third is the volume's closing cadence and stays.**

**The reviewer's judgement on the prohibitions is worth recording because it was made against the page and not against these files: all ten hold, the calendar is clean, the figures are not re-derived, the power state is held honestly, every chapter has a completed scene with a forward hook, and nothing stages a spent beat.**

## The third repair pass, and the review in `logs/batch-0001.review.log`
