## WHAT THIS BATCH IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**The water freed Thursday night goes down the long drain and stands in a lane on the far bank where a return has said *no inhabitants* for nineteen years, and a woman nineteen years in the third door is standing in it.** A washer is fitted, bread is carried, a certificate with a name in a box makes six doors countable, a Fair Copy magistrate seals two doors to protect a legal boundary and the bank gives way under the seal, an old flood book in a record house shows its last pages cut out with a pencil line fourteen years old and no name, a man admits a fourteen-year closure on a numbered sheet without giving names, a bank is held Sunday by four persons with a rope and a willow at their own request, and a flood court sits Monday and lays *persons accounted for* — eleven — where nineteen years said nobody. **And every evening a man fills a butt at a plank, forty trips, about an hour and sixteen minutes, and he cannot leave.**

## The five jobs, and where each stands

1. **Water somewhere new with a person in it:** done Ch 101–102. Lane to the top of a boot, six doors, pump and well under water. Explained nothing: no liar named, no finding, Dorrin kept out of Ch 101–103.
2. **Bend as social liability:** done Ch 103 (room refuses, rightly) and Ch 109 (bank holds because four say *we want it*). Difference unresolved by design.
3. **Dorrin and the Fair Copy, right about a thing:** done Ch 106–107, 110. Record not a lie, defect is nobody looked; seal protects boundary and makes a wave; right about water, wrong about valley; not villain, not rescued, procedure and deadline only.
4. **Record house and missing pages with a date:** done Ch 108. Flood-orders book, stubs, pencil line fourteen years, no signature, no return. Midpoint (conflicting orders as cause) set up, not delivered.
5. **Relationship change without repair:** done Ch 108–109. He admits closure to nine; she publishes the valley and holds his sheet. Trust damaged by honesty; condition four holds; nothing repaired.
