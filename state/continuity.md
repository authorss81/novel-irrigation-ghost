# Continuity State

## Baseline

- The story opens in drought-stricken Cresset, in the Marrow Basin. **Chapters 1–30 exist and are canon** (see `state/chapter-summaries.md`, `state/batch-0001-summary.md`, `state/batch-0002-summary.md`, and `state/batch-0003-summary.md`).
- Tarin Rusk is a 33-year-old senior flow engineer and the male protagonist. He is competent with ordinary hydraulics before any memory magic, and Chapters 1–4 show it on the page with no magic present.
- Glassstep was unlisted **twenty-six years** before the story, when Tarin was seven. Tarin's mother Lira Rusk disappeared during the breach; she later entered the One Map's galleries as a counter-reader and died there. No resurrection or return-as-guide is planned.
- The first opening event is residual pressurized water released behind a sealed inspection gate when Tarin opens it with a bar. Tarin does not create the water. **The head is 5·89 m and it is a real pressure head; the uphill run is a channel laid at a foot in the hundred.**
- **The run is a rising main, and it was designed to rise.** The covered terrace overflow leaves the Ashgate works at a gentle adverse grade and comes out at the low arch in Glassstep's yard above yard level, which is the whole of Chapter 1's "uphill." Water is *delivered* down this run; it does not *find* its way down it. That is why the lane's water stopped when the pump stopped.

## World and magic

- The drought is real. Unlisted routes are physically rerouted by the One Map's copper address plates and pressure-relief gates into the Blind Reservoir beneath the old Orisene works.
- A raw wet line can be sensed with physical contact. Deliberately naming or moving a route requires a physical trace, a willing witness, a durable record, and route legitimacy.
- Remembered hydraulics never creates water. It cannot read private thoughts or make false records harmless.
- Every deliberate use costs name debt. A private memory cannot be used as another person's anchor without informed consent; coercion creates a consent fracture.
- The Gauge is a limited, potentially wrong interface. It may state an immediate physical condition, never a complete solution or future probability.

## Relationships and factions

- Tarin and Mara Quill are the only primary romantic relationship. Mara is a non-magical record conservator with an independent legal and civic goal.
- Halden Roe is the local liar; Iven Marrow is the guild monopolist; Cael Dorrin is the legal eraser; Liora Tarn is the careful censor; Boryn Vey is the rational sacrificer and final human antagonist.
- The One Map is the inherited final external pressure. Boryn quietly reactivated its newest function six years before the story and expanded the drought protocol afterward; this is revealed publicly in Volume 5.

### Character and canon facts fixed in Batch 0003

- **Wick Venn, 38.** Two chapters in the batch, and the second is where he is the engine of the scene. **His left hand is the cost of the movement.** Left palm, grazed eight inches on a quarry face at the edge of a staging at about ten past one in the morning on the 6th while holding a ladder: full thickness off the heel of the hand and the base of the thumb, the outer two fingers white and cold, **no sensation in the outer two and none in the other three except under pressure**, cannot close the hand, cannot hold a rope or a bar, cannot hold a rail. **He can tie** — a becket, a bowline and a half hitch, with his knee on the rope and the tail in his teeth. - **Yara Sen.** Third chapter. She says the word **discharge** in a yard in daylight on purpose, before anybody can ask her a question, because a physician who answers a question has already lost the ground. She tells a yard to **go on selling** the tank to strangers and writes the hour on a slate so that in four weeks somebody can ask her why. She refuses the arithmetic of singling out one child. ## What Movement IV starts from — the state on the 15th

- **The lane's water: 950 gallons in the yard cistern, one foot seven inches, on the evening of the 14th.** Sales stopped after the 9th and 400 gallons were handed out in the yard. The three tanks and the pails are empty. The dispensary had **80 gallons** on the 13th and will draw 70 a day, so it is **gone on the 18th**, after four days. **There is not much more money to buy it with: £3 16s remains, which is 912 pence and buys 114 gallons at 8d or 76 gallons at a shilling — a day and a bit, and a cart.**
- **The pump has been stopped for thirteen days** and the run has been dry since the night of the 2nd. The only supply the lane has had since the 1st has been a contractor's **discharge**, and the district's public-health position, stated by Yara Sen in a yard on the 9th, is that **selling the tank to strangers beats drinking it.**
- **Sab Doulton, seven, is at the beginning of the fourth stage** with seventy gallons a day of a formula that no longer works. This is the seed of Movement IV's brine crosscurrent and it is a seed, not the flood.
- **The chamber is intact, measured, and known to four people and a district book.** 11 ft 1 in of standing water over the outside ground at the door of the works, under a street, with eleven houses and thirty-four persons on the roof of it.
- **The certificate is read and the finding is in four buildings** and is going up to the office that signed it.
- **The question of the row at Ashgate is on paper and unanswered:** *whether a district with no row may ask a settlement with no row for water.* **Thirty-four people, a rising well at 103 gallons an hour that has never been tested, a plant that has not been paid since the 2nd, and a certificate in a deal box saying the chamber is dry.** Nobody has told them any of it. Tarin has said in his own engineer's statement that he cannot promise the well will be the same water tomorrow.
- **Four of the five boxes on the warden's return are now in a book, and the fifth is empty on purpose.** *keeper, none held.* The route record needs a named route, a documented source, a witness and a date. **The date passed on the 22nd and is accepted. The documented source is a schedule reference and a chamber, which is not a document. The witness cannot be found. The keeper has not been asked, because the only man who could be asked has said no in front of eighty people and a warden, and is right.**
- **Two open charges, never joined, and a third forming.** And a district examiner who was due before the 27th of next month and whom the district has now asked to bring forward.
- **Tarin's position:** a trace, a cut record he cannot add to, a stone he cannot cut, two refusals he has made and one failure he cannot classify, a bad shoulder, a hand that will not seat, a cup of tea with no taste in it since the 7th, and a blue book with four and not one in it.
